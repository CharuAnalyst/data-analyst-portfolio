## Project Summary

This project presents an interactive **Call Center Performance Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel** to monitor call volume, generated amount, call duration, representative workload, customer satisfaction, and caller patterns.

The goal of this dashboard is to help operations teams answer:

> **How is the call center performing, which representatives are handling the workload, and where should service quality be reviewed?**

This README is written as an operations case study to show Excel dashboarding, KPI tracking, and business reporting judgment.

## Dashboard Walkthrough

### 1. Executive KPI View

The left-side KPI cards summarize total calls, amount generated, total duration, average rating, and happy caller count. This gives managers a quick view of operational health before reviewing detailed charts.

### 2. Call Trend Analysis

The monthly trend chart helps identify call activity movement across the year. The visible dashboard shows higher call activity around **March** and **October**, which may need staffing or campaign review.

### 3. Weekday Call Distribution

The weekday chart shows that call volume is not evenly distributed across the week. **Wednesday appears as the highest visible call-volume day**, which can help with scheduling and resource planning.

### 4. Representative Workload Review

The representative section compares call count and amount generated across R01 to R05. This helps identify workload concentration and whether some representatives are carrying more operational pressure than others.

### 5. Customer Satisfaction Review

The rating chart shows how customer satisfaction is distributed across rating levels. Ratings around **4 and 5 appear stronger**, but lower ratings should still be reviewed to understand service issues.

### 6. Caller Profile And Detail Matrix

The city/gender visual and customer matrix help management review caller patterns, customer-level values, and representative-level contribution in one place.

## Key Insights

### Stable Operational Volume

The dashboard tracks **1,000 calls**, giving a clear base for reviewing call center activity, representative workload, and customer satisfaction.

### Workload Needs Monitoring

Representative-level visuals show differences in call count and amount generated. This helps management check whether workload is balanced across the team.

### Wednesday Is A Scheduling Signal

Wednesday appears as the highest visible call-volume day. This can be used as a staffing signal for future workforce planning.

### Satisfaction Is Generally Positive

The average rating is **3.9**, and higher rating levels appear stronger in the rating chart. This suggests decent satisfaction, but lower-rated calls should be reviewed for service improvement.

### Dashboard Supports Management Review

The dashboard combines KPI cards, trend visuals, representative analysis, caller profile views, and a matrix report, making it useful for regular operational performance reviews.

---

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Review Representative Workload Balance

Compare call volume and amount generated across representatives to identify whether workload is evenly distributed or whether specific agents need support.

### 2. Plan Staffing Around Peak Days

Since Wednesday shows the highest visible call activity, managers should review staffing coverage and shift planning around mid-week demand.

### 3. Investigate Lower Ratings

Even with a good average rating, lower-rated calls should be reviewed to identify service gaps, call handling issues, or customer pain points.

### 4. Track Happy Caller Conversion

Happy caller count should be monitored with rating and call duration to understand whether faster calls, longer calls, or certain representatives drive better satisfaction.

### 5. Add Next-Level Metrics In Future

Future improvements could include average handling time, first-call resolution, abandoned calls, wait time, and representative-level satisfaction scores.
