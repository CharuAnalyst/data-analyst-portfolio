## Project Summary

This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard built in Power BI** to analyze revenue, quantity sold, product performance, country-wise contribution, and monthly sales movement.

The goal of this dashboard is to help business users quickly answer:

> **Which markets and products are driving sales, how revenue is trending over time, and what areas need deeper investigation?**

This README is written as a business case study to show not only dashboard creation, but also analytical thinking, KPI interpretation, and decision-focused reporting.

## Analysis Walkthrough

### 1. Revenue And Quantity Overview

The dashboard begins with executive KPI cards showing **21.74M total sales** and **2M total quantity sold**. These metrics give stakeholders a quick understanding of business scale before moving into deeper analysis.

### 2. Monthly Sales Trend

The monthly trend chart helps track sales movement across time. Sales show stronger periods around mid-2023 and early 2024, while May 2024 shows a sharp visible drop.

This drop should not be treated as a confirmed business decline without validation because the selected date range appears to end early in May.

### 3. Country-Level Sales Distribution

The country distribution view shows that the **UK and USA are the strongest visible revenue contributors**. This indicates that business performance is highly influenced by a few key markets.

### 4. Product Performance

The top product chart highlights products such as **Orange Choco, Drinking Coco, 99% Dark & Pure, Peanut Butter Cubes, and Organic Choco Syrup** as major contributors to sales.

This helps identify which products should be monitored closely for demand, stock availability, and promotion planning.

### 5. Product-Level Detail

The product table gives a more detailed view of sales amount by product. This supports deeper review of product dependency and helps compare high-performing items against the overall sales mix.

---

## Key Insights

### Strong Revenue Base

The dashboard reports **21.74M in total sales**, showing a strong sales base across the selected period.

### Revenue Is Market-Concentrated

The UK and USA contribute a major visible share of sales. This means changes in these markets could significantly affect overall revenue performance.

### Product Sales Are Concentrated

A small group of products appears to drive a large portion of revenue. This is useful for identifying priority products, but it also creates dependency risk if demand drops for those items.

### May 2024 Needs Validation

The dashboard shows a sharp drop in May 2024. Since the visible date range appears to include only the beginning of May, this should be treated as a **partial-period data check**, not an immediate performance failure.

---

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Validate Partial Month Data

Before making decisions based on the May 2024 drop, confirm whether the month contains complete sales data. If not, the dashboard should label it as a partial month.

### 2. Monitor Key Markets Closely

Since the UK and USA contribute strongly to revenue, sales teams should monitor these markets for demand changes, pricing shifts, and product availability issues.

### 3. Review Product Dependency

High-performing products should be reviewed for stock planning, promotional strategy, and revenue risk. Heavy dependency on a few products can make sales performance vulnerable.

### 4. Compare Sales With Quantity

Total quantity sold is high, but quantity alone does not explain revenue quality. Product pricing, product mix, and market contribution should be reviewed together.

### 5. Add Profitability Metrics In Future

The next improvement would be to add profit, margin, discount, and cost metrics so the dashboard can support stronger commercial decision-making.
